Health Notices & News

Latest health activities, awareness programs, and medical updates from Nepal

Free Health Camp in Kathmandu

July 2025

The Ministry of Health Nepal organized a free health camp in Kathmandu focusing on blood pressure, diabetes screening, and general checkups. Over 2,000 citizens benefited from this initiative.

Dengue Awareness Campaign

June 2025

With rising dengue cases during monsoon, health authorities launched an awareness campaign across major cities, educating people on mosquito control and early symptoms.

New Government Hospital Wing Opened

May 2025

A new emergency and trauma care wing was inaugurated at Bir Hospital, improving emergency services and reducing patient load in Kathmandu valley hospitals.

Mental Health Awareness Week

April 2025

Nepal observed Mental Health Awareness Week with seminars, free counseling sessions, and online webinars to reduce stigma and promote psychological well-being.

Vaccination Drive for Children

March 2025

Nationwide vaccination programs were conducted to immunize children against measles, rubella, and polio, especially in rural and remote areas.

Digital Health Records Initiative

February 2025

The Government of Nepal announced a digital health record system to improve patient data management and coordination among hospitals and clinics.
