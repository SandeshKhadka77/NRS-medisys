About Our Hospital

History

Founded in 2025, NRS MediSys -- It is college Project started with the vision of providing affordable, high-quality healthcare to everyone. We began as a small clinic and have now grown into a multi-specialty hospital.

Achievements

  • National Healthcare Excellence Award - 2025
  • More than 100 successful surgeries
  • ISO 9001 Certified Hospital

Future Goals

Our goal is to expand our telemedicine services to rural Nepal,
establish a new medical college, and launch advanced robotic surgery units by 2030.

Hospital Alumni

We are proud of our alumni doctors who now serve in top hospitals globally.
Their achievements inspire our next generation of medical professionals.
